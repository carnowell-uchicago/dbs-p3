# Onboarding Research

Step 3 of the brief: study how three similar tools welcome someone new (the first screen, what they ask for, and how soon you get something useful), then decide what to use in our first visit. Three subagents researched these tools on 2026-10-18 from public pages, store listings, and UX teardowns. None of them signed up for anything.

## Duolingo (daily habit loop)
- **First screen:** "Learn for free. Forever." with Get Started and I already have an account. The mascot then says "Just a few quick questions before your first lesson."
- **What it asks for:**
  - About 7–10 questions: language, level, why you're learning, and a daily goal labeled as an identity ("Casual" through "Intense", with an "I'm committed" button).
  - It also asks how you heard about it, and current builds show a paywall.
  - **The account comes after the first lesson**, framed as "Save your progress." Reported effect: about 20% more daily active users (secondhand figure).
- **Time to value:** first tap in about 3s, first real exercise in about 90s, after 12–25 screens.
- **Return:**
  - **Streaks:** users with a 7-day streak are 3.6× more likely to finish, and the motivation becomes loss aversion.
  - **Streak freezes**, and reminders timed to the user's usual hour.
  - **Guilt notifications** ("You made Duo sad"), which became widely criticized as manipulative.
- **Sources:** gummble.com/blog/duolingo-onboarding-flow-analysis, goodux.appcues.com/blog/duolingo-user-onboarding, blog.duolingo.com/how-duolingo-streak-builds-habit, botsi.com/resources/flows/apps/duolingo

## Happyfeed (now "Orca", a gratitude diary)
- **First screen:** an account form (name, email, password, or Apple sign-in) **before any value**, framed as keeping moments safe across devices.
- **What it asks for:** name, email, password, and an optional reminder time. A sharing prompt appears after entries, and reviewers dislike it.
- **Time to value:** right after sign-up you can log "3 good things."
- **Return:**
  - Daily reminder, streaks, weekly and monthly recaps.
  - Progressive unlocks: throwbacks to the same date a week, month, or year ago.
  - **"Happiness Jar"** (after 2 weeks): shake the phone to see a random past day.
  - Many past entries are behind the paid tier.
- **Sources:** okorca.com, okorca.com/blog/happyfeed-is-becoming-orca, apps.apple.com/us/app/orca-formerly-happyfeed-diary/id707073943, okorca.com/plus-faq

## Random Acts of Kindness Foundation (randomactsofkindness.org)
- **First screen:** "We provide FREE kindness resources to help you make kindness the norm," followed by navigation for school, home, and work, and a mascot ("Norm").
- **What it asks for:** nothing. Ideas and printables are open, and email sign-up (RAKtivist, "Daily Dose") is opt-in.
- **Time to value:** one click to an idea. The idea pages are just a title, a photo, and share buttons, with no how-to.
- **Return:** weekday quote emails, a yearly 365-act printable calendar, 10-day challenge booklets, Kindness Bingo, and RAK Week.
- **Weakness:** no way to mark an act done, log it, reflect, personalize it, or skip it.
- **Sources:** randomactsofkindness.org, /kindness-ideas, /printables, /daily-dose-of-kindness

## Other kindness apps found (avoid resembling)
- **Acts of Kindness: Missions:** daily act cards, a streak with shields, a companion that grows, a scrapbook.
- **be nice: daily kindness:** one goal a day with scripts, mood before and after, a streak.
- **30 Acts of Kindness:** green tiles fill as you go. **Kindness Chain:** logging grows a virtual plant, plus badges.
- **BeKind:** ideas, plus stories and photos. **Gratitude Jar:** a jar metaphor for gratitude.

## What to avoid copying
- **Duolingo:** a talking mascot, flames, the word "streak," freezes, XP, hearts, leagues, identity labels for goals, guilt-trip reminders, and bright green.
- **Happyfeed:** "Happiness/Gratitude Jar," shake to see a random past entry, "3 good things," Throwbacks/Moments/Pods/Waves, and a photo-first feed.
- **RAK:** RAKtivist, "Make kindness the norm," Daily Dose, Kindness Calendar/Challenge/Bingo, a one-act-per-day calendar grid, ideas divided by school/home/work, and doodle illustrations.
- **RAK prompt wording:** "Give a genuine compliment to three people today," "Text or message someone who helped you just to say thanks," "Be kind to people in the service industry," "Share an uplifting song," and "Write three things you love about yourself."
- **Other kindness apps:** growing plants or pets, tile grids that fill up, and streak shields.
